# A course in motion

## Request and authority

The user rejected the explanatory layout and explicitly requested a highly graphical, visually impressive website with movement carrying a story through the tools. This supersedes the static network composition. Product authority remains the reviewed portfolio and public distribution evidence. English/Spanish routes and factual product guides remain intact.

## THESIS

One teaching idea becomes a course: shape it, establish assessment criteria, review submissions, observe the course, and reuse its content. The visitor follows that course rather than browsing an equal-weight repository catalog.

## OWN-WORLD

Evolve the existing coral/carbon/concrete identity into a large editorial stage. Space Grotesk supplies structure; Instrument Serif supplies the idea's expressive voice. Peach, ochre, lilac, mint and coral stage grounds distinguish the course's changing needs. Graphics are authored interface diagrams and geometry, not photographs or fabricated application screenshots.

## STORY

Monumental opening and exploded course composition → short narrative bridge → five chapters sharing a transforming sticky course scene → a graphic destination and practical starting links → concise FAQ. Each chapter links to the real product guides when its problem arises. Setup and availability remain visible near those links.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

“One idea. A whole course.” / “Una idea. Todo un curso.” Large sans/italic serif typography with layered, perspective-transformed course modules in an orange disc composition. A short introduction and a real anchor begin the journey. No fake chat, simulated execution result or invented student data.

## FORM AND MOTION

- Focal moment: a persistent course stage changes from outline to rubric, feedback, activity signals and cross-site reuse as the corresponding chapter is read.
- Continuity: five chapter anchors and a progress rail show where the reader is; native scrolling controls pacing without interception.
