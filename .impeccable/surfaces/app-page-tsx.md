# Cinematic homepage surface

The current homepage is the bilingual server-rendered composition in app/home.tsx. Its design authority is docs/cinematic-direction.md and DESIGN.md. It uses Taste Skill's Loud and flat direction and GSAP ScrollTrigger Storytelling, with independent product requirements and public availability preserved from PRODUCT.md and docs/portfolio-strategy.md.

The old connected-network homepage was rejected by the user. Earlier contracts and .impeccable/review captures are historical evidence. Current visual and browser checks are under .taste-skill/. Homepage copy is in app/cinematic-copy.ts, motion in app/story-motion.tsx, artefacts in app/course-art.tsx and styles in app/cinematic.css. Guide pages retain their prior visual tokens and system dark theme.
